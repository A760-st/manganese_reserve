# Viva Q&A

**Can satellite imagery find underground ore?** No. It supplies surface/environmental/geological proxy features; subsurface evidence requires drill holes, samples, geochemistry, geophysics, or geological models.

**What is the platform's role?** Decision support: it organizes evidence, predicts prospectivity and shortfall risk, explains model contributions, and evaluates scenarios for human review.

**Why label data?** To prevent demo fixtures from being mistaken for authorized operational or certified geological information.
